## What Salira Needs to Add (Priority Order)

### HIGH PRIORITY (Do This Now)

1. **Phone number in header** — Indian clients want to call. Add a sales number.
2. **WhatsApp floating button** — Every Indian business website has this. Add it.
3. **Contact form on homepage** — Not just email. A form with name, phone, message.
4. **"Since 2024" or founding year** — Establish credibility. Even 1 year is fine.
5. **Project count stat** — "10+ projects delivered" or whatever the real number is.
6. **Physical address** — Even a registered office address builds trust.
7. **"Why Choose Us" section** — Explicit 4–5 reasons to pick Salira.

### MEDIUM PRIORITY (Do This Next)

8. **More portfolio items** — Need at least 5–8 pieces. Even concept work counts.
9. **Client testimonials** — Even 2–3 quotes from past clients.
10. **Service breakdown pages** — "Web Development", "Mobile Apps", "Custom Software" as separate sections or pages.
11. **Industry pages** — Show you work across industries (not just restaurants).
12. **Review badges** — Google, Clutch, GoodFirms (even if you start with 1 review).
13. **Blog** — Even 2–3 articles establish expertise.

### LOW PRIORITY (Do Later)

14. **Careers page** — Shows growth and legitimacy.
15. **Refund policy page** — Professional touch.
16. **Terms & Conditions page** — Legal credibility.
17. **Privacy policy page** — Required for trust.
18. **Dedicated pricing page** — Separate from homepage.

## Summary

**WebbiTech feels standard because:**
- Trust signals everywhere (years, projects, logos, ratings)
- 5 ways to contact (phone, WhatsApp, email, form, address)
- Simple pricing with clear tiers
- Lots of portfolio examples
- FAQ answering every objection
- Multiple CTAs on every section

**Salira feels like a startup because:**
- No trust signals (no years, no projects, no logos, no ratings)
- Only email contact (no phone, WhatsApp, form)
- Pricing is detailed but no "POPULAR" / "GET A QUOTE" labels
- Only 1 portfolio piece
- Fewer CTAs
- No "Why Choose Us" pitch

**The fix is not to copy WebbiTech. The fix is to add the trust and accessibility elements that make a visitor feel safe choosing you.**
