### SECTION 9: FAQ (Answer Every Objection)

**Q: I don't know what I need technically. Is that okay?**
> Yes. That's exactly why we exist. Tell us your business problem in plain language. We'll figure out the technical part and explain it back to you in simple terms before you pay anything.

**Q: Why should I trust you with my money?**
> You don't pay everything upfront. You pay in milestones — 30% to start, 30% at design approval, 20% at midpoint, 20% on launch. You see real work before each payment. And you own all the code from Day 1.

**Q: What if I need changes after launch?**
> Every project includes free support (30 days to 6 months depending on package). After that, maintenance plans start at ₹8,000/month. You can also hire any developer to make changes — you own the code.

**Q: How is this different from hiring a freelancer?**
> Freelancers are individuals. We're a team. You get design + development + testing + project management. Fixed timeline. Fixed price. Someone to call when things break. And we don't disappear mid-project.

**Q: Can I start small and add features later?**
> Absolutely. Start with the Starter package. When you're ready for an app or booking system, add it as a separate project. We build in a way that makes adding features easy later.

**Q: Do you build for specific industries?**
> We build for any business that needs software. Restaurants, travel companies, clinics, retail stores, education institutes, logistics companies, salons, gyms — if you have a business process, we can digitize it.

**Q: What about hosting and domain costs?**
> Hosting for the first year is included in your project price. After that, it's ₹3,000–15,000/year depending on traffic. Domain is ₹800–1,500/year. We'll set everything up for you.

**Q: How do I know the price is fair?**
> Our prices are based on actual Indian market rates from 300+ projects. We're not the cheapest (that's freelancers who may disappear). We're not the most expensive (that's large agencies with overhead). We're the sweet spot: quality work, fair price, reliable delivery.

**Q: What if my project costs more than the package?**
> The prices shown are starting prices. Your final quote depends on your specific requirements. We'll give you an exact number after a free 30-minute discovery call. No commitment.

**Q: Can I pay in EMI?**
> Yes. For projects above ₹3L, we offer EMI options through Razorpay. Pay in 3–6 monthly installments.

## Page Copy Rules

### Do's
- Use ₹ symbols and Indian number formatting (₹1,49,999 not $1799)
- Show "onwards" so clients know it's a starting point
- Use simple language — "app" not "cross-platform mobile application"
- Show what's NOT included to set expectations
- Use real Indian business examples (restaurant, travel, clinic)
- Show payment milestones so clients feel safe
- Include EMI option for projects above ₹3L

### Don'ts
- Never say "contact us for pricing" — show starting prices
- Never use tech jargon (React, Flutter, Node.js, API)
- Never hide costs or add hidden fees
- Never promise timelines you can't keep
- Never compare yourself to freelancers negatively
- Never use "Gold/Silver/Bronze" tier names
