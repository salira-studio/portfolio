## How to Quote a Project

### Step 1: Discovery Call (Free, 30–60 min)
Ask these questions:
1. What does your business do?
2. What problem are you trying to solve?
3. Who will use this? (customers, employees, both?)
4. Do you need a website, mobile app, or both?
5. What features are must-haves vs nice-to-haves?
6. Do you have a budget in mind?
7. What's your timeline?
8. Do you have existing systems? (POS, CRM, payment gateway)

### Step 2: Scope Document (Paid or Free depending on project size)
- List all features with priority (Must / Should / Could)
- Define user roles (customer, admin, staff, etc.)
- List integrations needed
- Define success criteria

### Step 3: Quote Structure
Always present as fixed-price with milestones. Never hourly for Indian clients.

**Payment Milestones (Recommended):**

| Milestone | % of Total | When |
|-----------|------------|------|
| Advance | 30% | Project start |
| Design Approval | 20% | After design is approved |
| Development Midpoint | 25% | When core features are demo-able |
| Final Delivery | 25% | After UAT and launch |

For projects under ₹3L: 50% advance, 50% on delivery (simpler).

### Step 4: Presenting the Quote

**Always show:**
- Total project price (fixed)
- Timeline (weeks)
- What's included (feature list)
- What's NOT included (scope boundaries)
- Payment schedule
- Warranty period (30–90 days free support after launch)

**Never:**
- Quote hourly without a cap
- Say "it depends" without a range
- Hide costs or add hidden fees
- Skip the scope document

## Pricing Psychology for Indian Clients

### Do's
- Show the value first: "This app will handle 500 orders/day automatically"
- Use round numbers: ₹5,00,000 not ₹4,87,500
- Offer 2–3 options (Basic / Standard / Premium) — let them choose
- Mention EMI options for projects above ₹3L
- Show social proof: "We built X for Y, here's the result"
- Always mention GST separately: "₹5,00,000 + 18% GST = ₹5,90,000"
- Include a warranty: "30 days free support after launch"

### Don'ts
- Don't quote too low — it signals poor quality
- Don't negotiate on scope — negotiate on timeline or features
- Don't give discounts without getting something (faster payment, referral, case study)
- Don't start work without 30% advance
- Don't promise "unlimited revisions" — define revision rounds (2–3 rounds)

### Discount Rules
| Discount Type | When | Amount |
|---------------|------|--------|
| Full payment upfront | Client pays 100% at start | 5–10% off |
| Referral | Existing client refers new client | ₹10,000–₹25,000 off or credit |
| Bundle | Client buys website + app together | 10–15% off combined price |
| Annual retainer | Client commits to 12-month maintenance | 10–15% off retainer |
| First project | New client, building trust | 5% max (never more) |

## GST & Tax Notes

- All prices are **pre-GST**
- GST rate for IT services: **18%**
- If client is GST-registered: they claim input tax credit (no extra cost to them)
- If client is NOT GST-registered: 18% is additional cost
- TDS: If client is under tax audit, they deduct 10% TDS under Section 194J
- Always issue GST-compliant invoices
