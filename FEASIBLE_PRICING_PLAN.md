## The Real Money: Maintenance Retainers

This is how you survive and grow. One-time projects pay the bills. Maintenance retainers keep you alive.

| Plan | Monthly | Annual | What's Included |
|------|---------|--------|----------------|
| **Basic** | ₹5,000 – ₹10,000 | ₹50,000 – ₹1,00,000 | Bug fixes, security updates, hosting monitoring |
| **Standard** | ₹10,000 – ₹20,000 | ₹1,00,000 – ₹2,00,000 | Everything above + content changes, monthly reports |
| **Premium** | ₹20,000 – ₹40,000 | ₹2,00,000 – ₹4,00,000 | Everything above + new features, priority support |

**The math:**
- 10 clients on ₹10,000/month maintenance = ₹1,00,000/month recurring
- 20 clients on ₹10,000/month maintenance = ₹2,00,000/month recurring
- This is your salary, your runway, your survival

**When to sell maintenance:**
- Always. Every project should include 3–6 months of maintenance in the price
- After that, convert to monthly retainer
- Never deliver a project without a maintenance plan

## Project Mix (What to Actually Sell)

### Tier 1: Quick Wins (₹40K–₹1.5L) — 40% of projects
- Business websites (5–15 pages)
- Landing pages for ads
- Portfolio sites
- Simple WordPress/Wix alternatives

**Why:** Fast to deliver (1–3 weeks), high volume, builds reputation, gets referrals

### Tier 2: Core Projects (₹1.5L–₹4L) — 40% of projects
- E-commerce stores
- Business apps (Flutter)
- CRM/dashboard builds
- Booking systems

**Why:** Your bread and butter. Good margin, reasonable timeline, repeatable

### Tier 3: Premium Builds (₹4L–₹10L) — 20% of projects
- Full-stack platforms
- Multi-app systems
- Complex web applications
- Enterprise dashboards

**Why:** Higher margin, but harder to close. Don't depend on these

## How to Sell at These Prices

### Don't Compete On Price — Compete On Value

❌ "We're cheaper than agencies"
✅ "We deliver the same quality at a fair price because we're lean"

❌ "Our prices are low"
✅ "Our prices are honest — no hidden fees, no surprise bills"

### What To Say When Clients Push Back

**"That's too expensive"**
> "What's your budget? I can adjust the scope to fit. A focused website at ₹80K that converts visitors is better than a ₹3L site that doesn't."

**"Freelancer quoted ₹30K"**
> "That's a fair price for freelance work. We offer agency-grade process, fixed timelines, warranty, and someone to call when things break. Different value, different price."

**"Can you do it for less?"**
> "I can remove [specific feature] to bring it to ₹X. Or we can start with Phase 1 now and add Phase 2 later when budget allows."

### EMI Option (For Projects Above ₹3L)
> "We offer EMI options through [Razorpay/Cashfree]. You can pay in 3–6 monthly installments. Many of our clients prefer this."

## The Bottom Line

**Don't aim for 20 big clients. Aim for 30+ smaller clients.**

- ₹40K websites get you in the door
- ₹1.5L–3L apps are your bread and butter
- ₹5K–15K/month maintenance keeps you alive
- Volume + retention = ₹1 Crore

**Your competitive advantage:**
- Not the cheapest (that's freelancers)
- Not the most expensive (that's agencies)
- The sweet spot: quality work, fair price, reliable delivery

**The trap to avoid:**
- Don't quote ₹10L+ projects hoping for a whale
- Don't work for free to "build portfolio"
- Don't skip maintenance contracts
- Don't start work without advance payment
