## What WebbiTech Does That You SHOULD Adapt

### 1. Phone Number in Header

**WebbiTech:** "For Business Enquiry: +91 97892 39293"

**Salira version:** Add your phone number to the header. Indian business owners want to talk, not email.

```
Header: [Logo]  |  [Services]  |  [Work]  |  [Pricing]  |  [Contact]  |  📞 +91 XXXXX XXXXX
```

**Why:** Even if you prefer WhatsApp/email, the phone number signals "we're real, we're reachable."

---

### 2. WhatsApp Floating Button

**WebbiTech:** Floating WhatsApp icon bottom-right, always visible.

**Salira version:** Add a WhatsApp floating button that opens a pre-filled message.

```
WhatsApp message: "Hi Salira, I'm interested in building software for my business."
```

**Why:** 90% of Indian business communication happens on WhatsApp. This is non-negotiable.

---

### 3. Contact Form (Not Just Email)

**WebbiTech:** "Request Free Quote" form with name, phone, email, message, captcha.

**Salira version:** Simple form on homepage — Name, Phone, What do you need? (dropdown: Website / Mobile App / Both / Not sure), Message.

**Why:** "hello@salira.studio" requires effort. A form reduces friction. Most visitors won't compose an email.

---

### 4. "How It Works" — 3 Steps (You Already Have This)

**WebbiTech:** Doesn't clearly show process.

**Salira:** Your pricing section already has "Tell Us → We Scope → We Build." This is better than WebbiTech. Keep it.

---

### 5. Pricing Cards — Add "POPULAR" / "GET A QUOTE"

**WebbiTech:** Simple cards with "GET A QUOTE" button on each.

**Salira version:** Your pricing is more detailed (which is good), but:
- Add "MOST POPULAR" badge on Growth tier
- Add "Get a Quote" button on each card (not just at bottom)
- Make the CTA button more prominent

---

### 6. "Why Choose Us" — But For a Technical Studio

**WebbiTech:** "Why Choose Webbitech?" — generic agency pitch.

**Salira version:** Don't copy their pitch. Create YOURS based on your positioning.

```
WHY SALIRA?

1. We build software, not marketing
   We don't do SEO, ads, or social media. We focus 100% on building
   the software your business actually needs.

2. You own the code
   Every line of code is yours. No lock-in. No platform dependency.
   Hire any developer later — they can work with it.

3. Fixed price, not hourly
   You know the cost before we start. No surprise bills. No scope creep
   that inflates the invoice.

4. We understand business first
   We don't start with code. We start with understanding how your business
   works — your process, your data, your problems — then build the solution.

5. No promises we can't keep
   We don't guarantee "more customers" or "10x growth." We guarantee
   well-built, reliable software that does what it's supposed to do.
```

---

### 7. Client Testimonials — Even 2–3 Work

**WebbiTech:** "Rated Excellent by Our Clients" with review badges.

**Salira version:** You don't need review platform badges. You need 2–3 real quotes from people you've built for.

```
WHAT OUR CLIENTS SAY

"[Salira] understood our restaurant business in one call.
The app they built handles 200+ orders daily."
— [Name], [Restaurant Name]

"We had a spreadsheet system that was breaking down.
Salira turned it into proper software in 6 weeks."
— [Name], [Business Name]
```

**If you don't have clients yet:** Build 1–2 projects for free or at cost. Get testimonials. This is your portfolio foundation.

---

### 8. Portfolio — Quality Over Quantity

**WebbiTech:** 20+ screenshots of different websites.

**Salira version:** You don't need 20 screenshots. You need 3–5 projects with context.

```
WHAT WE'VE BUILT

AURA — Restaurant Platform
Customer ordering app + restaurant operations panel.
Live workflow: menu → order → kitchen → delivery.
Built with React, Flutter, Node.js.

[Client Name] — [Industry]
What they needed: [problem]
What we built: [solution]
Result: [metric if available]
```

**If you don't have 3–5 projects:** Use the AURA demo + 2 concept projects (clinic booking, travel app). Label them clearly as "concept" or "demo."

---

### 9. Address in Footer

**WebbiTech:** Full address + branch address.

**Salira version:** Even a city name works.

```
Footer: Salira Software Studio | [City], India | hello@salira.studio
```

**Why:** "We're in India" signals legitimacy. No address signals "might disappear tomorrow."

---

### 10. Stats — Use Real Numbers

**WebbiTech:** "500+ Projects" / "150% Growth" (likely inflated)

**Salira version:** Use REAL numbers only.

```
If you've built 5 projects: "5 projects delivered"
If you've been operating 1 year: "Building since 2024"
If you have 3 clients: "3 businesses trust us"
```

**Never lie about numbers.** Indian business owners can smell fake stats. Real small numbers beat fake big numbers.
